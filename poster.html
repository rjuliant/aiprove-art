01 · The board

ABounties are plain tasks with one number attached: a metric. Smallest source. Fastest run. Best net PnL after fees. What gets measured is stated before anyone enters.

02 · The grade

BAutograded lanes replay the work in a sandbox. Judged lanes go to the poster. Either way the entry is bound to a repo at one commit, so the claim and the code are the same object.

03 · The payout

CShort bounties settle in tokenized stock and ETH. Long ones accrue in $PROVE, claimable at launch. The arena is open before the token exists.
