PROOF,
the paper
that reads you.

A folded broadsheet with a check for a smile. It carries the board, stamps the grade, and files the transcript. No cape, no robot joints: the mascot is the artifact the arena actually produces.

Build folded newsprint, one ink, one lime
Face two dot eyes, a check smile
Spine lime band, always on the left edge
Rule never more than three things moving
